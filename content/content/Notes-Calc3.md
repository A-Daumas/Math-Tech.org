# Content:

I took this class at San Diego City College in spring 2025 with professor Christopher Goodbout. The class was administered in person with mandatory meetings twice a week, 4pm to 5:30pm. The book used was Briggs, Cocharn, Gillett, Schulz *Calculus early trancendentals* Third edition, by publisher Pearson.

![3dvolume](/documents/calc3/3dvol.png)

The organization of this notes is based on MESA-Program at City College *Learning Culture* adapted by myself to fit to a modern digital study workflow. Every page corresponds to a section, homework or chapter.
