# Content:

I took this class at San Diego City College in fall 2025 with professor Christopher Goodbout. The class was administered fully online with mandatory meetings twice a week, 5pm to 7pm. The book used was Nagle, Staff and Snider *Fundamentals Of Differential Equations* Ninth edition, published by Pearson. Important to note, in this class we used the Pearson online software for homemorks.

![Graph](/documents/Differential/diff.jpg)

The organization of this notes is based on MESA-Program at City College *Learning Culture* adapted by myself to fit to a modern digital study workflow. Every page corresponds to a section, homework or chapter.
